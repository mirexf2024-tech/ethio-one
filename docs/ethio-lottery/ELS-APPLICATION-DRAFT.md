# ELS Application — Draft Only / Not Submitted

**Applicant model:** own raffle operator, subject to written legal confirmation.  
**Proposed first category:** raffle / tombola.  
**Status:** internal draft only. Do not send or submit.

## Request

To: Ethiopian Lottery Service / National Lottery Administration  
Subject: Request for legal classification and own-operator raffle licensing requirements for Ethio One

Ethio One is an Ethiopia-focused digital services platform. The project is assessing the feasibility of operating a digital raffle through its own legally registered operating entity, subject to all applicable laws, licences, directives and regulatory approvals.

Before beginning any application or public activity, we request written clarification of:

1. The exact legal classification of the proposed digital raffle / tombola under current Ethiopian law and directives.
2. Whether an independently operated digital raffle is eligible for an own-operator permit, and the legal entity requirements.
3. Current application forms, eligibility, ownership, capital, tax registration, financial guarantees, licence fees, renewal and reporting requirements.
4. Whether the app, ticket design, ticket numbering, sales workflow, draw process and prize distribution require prior technical approval.
5. Required draw supervision, independent audit, randomness certification, prize reserve, winner verification and dispute procedures.
6. The statutory commission, tax treatment, fees and other financial obligations applicable to this exact category.
7. Whether ELS approval is sufficient or additional approvals from other authorities are required.
8. Whether CBE, Telebirr and M-PESA may be used for the licensed activity and which separate provider approvals are required.

Ethio One will not sell tickets, accept funds, conduct a paid draw or advertise a paid raffle before all required written approvals are in place.
