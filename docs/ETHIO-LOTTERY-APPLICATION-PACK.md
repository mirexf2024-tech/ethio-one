## Letter to Ethiopian Lottery Service / National Lottery Administration

**Subject: Request for Own Digital Raffle Licensing Guidance and Application Requirements**

Dear Sir/Madam,

We are preparing Ethio Lottery, a proposed digital raffle service within the Ethio One platform. We intend to operate it through our own legally registered entity, subject to the applicable Ethiopian laws, licences, directives and written approvals.

We respectfully request written guidance on:

1. The legal classification of the proposed digital raffle and the applicable licensing category.
2. Whether our entity is eligible to apply for an own-operator raffle licence.
3. Current application forms, eligibility requirements, required corporate documents, capital/financial requirements, statutory fees and renewal conditions.
4. Whether separate approvals are required for online ticket issuance, QR verification, digital payments, electronic draw procedures, results publication and prize distribution.
5. The applicable statutory commission, tax treatment, prize-fund safeguarding and reporting obligations.
6. Requirements for draw supervision, independent audit, random-number generation, winner verification, dispute resolution and record retention.
7. Whether each raffle campaign and its rules must be approved before publication.
8. Whether CBE, Telebirr and M-PESA may be used for transactions under an approved raffle licence, and whether each provider must obtain or issue a separate written authorisation.
9. Any additional approval required from another government authority.

We will not sell tickets, accept payments, conduct prize-bearing draws or advertise a paid raffle before all required approvals have been obtained and verified.

Please provide the official application procedure, relevant current directives and the responsible department/contact.
